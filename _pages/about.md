Welcome to the research group of Dr. Leiting Zhang (张雷霆) at the [Ångström Advanced Battery Centre](https://www.uu.se/en/department/chemistry-angstrom-laboratory/research/structural-chemistry), Uppsala University. We study battery electrodes, electrolytes, and interphases, and develop *operando* and online methods for assessing sustainable and smart batteries. Learn more about our work on the [Research](/research/) page.
